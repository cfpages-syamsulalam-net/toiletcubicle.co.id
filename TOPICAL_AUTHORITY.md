# Topical Authority — toiletcubicle.co.id

## Role and boundary

`ToiletCubicle.co.id` is the specialist commercial and educational property for toilet-partition systems: planning, materials, hardware, fabrication, installation, hygiene, accessibility, maintenance, and procurement. Existing routes also advertise public toilets, portable/movable toilets, urinal screens, shower boxes, lockers, worktops, and other fabricated products. Those adjacencies need explicit boundaries so they support rather than dilute the core entity.

Owned `Kubikel.co.id`, `Kubikel.id`, `Cubicle.co.id`, `Cubicle.id`, `ToiletPhenolic.co.id`, `Phenolic.id`, and `Partisi.co.id` may independently cover the same queries; cross-domain overlap is not cannibalization. Within this domain, one page must own each intent. `/`, material routes, building-use routes, product routes, and contact routes keep commercial intent. Structural, accessibility, hygiene/health, glass, fire, K3, and standards claims require current primary evidence and competent review.
